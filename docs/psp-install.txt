MM PSP installation

1. Extract this archive and copy the MM_PSP folder to PSP/GAME on your
   PSP storage:
   - PSP Go internal storage: ef0:/PSP/GAME/MM_PSP/
   - Memory Stick or PSP Go M2 card: ms0:/PSP/GAME/MM_PSP/
   Keep the Plugins and data folders beside EBOOT.PBP. The same package works
   on either device.

2. Put your own big-endian, compressed US Majora's Mask ROM in
   PSP/GAME/MM_PSP/data/basrom.z64. The ROM must be 32 MiB with CRC32 B428D8A7.
   The filename baserom.z64 is also accepted.

3. Launch MM PSP Port from the PSP Game menu. On first launch, wait for ROM
   validation and asset conversion to finish. Later launches reuse the generated
   data/segments/oot_psp_assets.bin file.

Installed layout:

PSP/GAME/MM_PSP/
    EBOOT.PBP
    README.txt
    Plugins/dvemgr.prx
    data/basrom.z64       (add your own ROM)

Plugins/dvemgr.prx enables TV output on supported PSP models. Open HOME >
Video Settings to configure it. MM requires PSP-2000/3000/Go expanded memory; PSP-1000 is unsupported.

To regenerate assets, delete data/segments/oot_psp_assets.bin and launch again
with the ROM still present.

The ROM, converted asset pack, mm-psp-save.bin, controls.ini, video.ini, and
sleep recovery data all use the storage containing the launched EBOOT. For
example, a Go internal installation reads ef0:/PSP/GAME/MM_PSP/data/basrom.z64.
Each installation keeps its own saves and settings. To move an installation
between internal storage and a card, copy the whole MM_PSP folder.

The archive includes the compiled port and its DVE module. You supply the ROM;
no ROM or converted game assets are included in this download.

HOME > Video Settings > Frame Rate selects 20 FPS (default) or 60 FPS.
Select Save video.ini to keep the setting. Game logic retains its original
update rate; 60 FPS adds interpolated presentations. LCD/240p use triple
buffering; 480i/480p keep double buffering to fit VRAM. Actual FPS depends on
the scene and hardware. See docs/psp-port.md in the source tree.
